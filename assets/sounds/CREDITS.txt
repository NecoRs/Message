Sound effects
=============

All files in this folder are derived from two packs by Kenney (www.kenney.nl),
released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0):
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

  Interface Sounds (1.0)  https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
  Sci-Fi Sounds           https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds

They are free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects.
Attribution is not required; it is given here as thanks.

The files were trimmed, faded, peak-normalised and encoded as mono MP3 by
tools/sounds/build_sounds.py. The two loops were made seamless with a
crossfade, and "execute" layers glitch_001 with select_002.

A dark, menacing "black-hat" set. Every sound is pitched down and driven
into soft distortion (with tails / a dread sub-layer) by build_sounds.py for
a deeper, dirtier, more ominous character. Sources:

  type-1..5.mp3        glitch_001, glitch_002, glitch_004, glitch_003, tick_002
  tick.mp3             glitch_003
  row-1, row-2.mp3     glitch_004, drop_002
  blip.mp3             glitch_002
  execute.mp3          glitch_002 + laser_retro_000 (Sci-Fi)
  success-1, -2.mp3    confirmation_002, confirmation_003
  warning.mp3          impact_metal_004 (Sci-Fi)
  error.mp3            impact_metal_002 (Sci-Fi)
  sweep-1, -2.mp3      force_field_000, force_field_002 (Sci-Fi)
  sweep-3.mp3          low_frequency_explosion_000 (Sci-Fi)
  stinger.mp3          impact_metal_001 (Sci-Fi) — scene-change horror hit
  boot.mp3             low_frequency_explosion_001 (Sci-Fi)
  loop-processing.mp3  computer_noise_002 (Sci-Fi)
  loop-ambience.mp3    space_engine_low_001 (Sci-Fi) + dread sub-layer
  radio-1..5.mp3       fictional police-style dispatch lines spoken by meSpeak,
                       wrapped in a synthesised squelch, static and roger beep

Voices (rendered offline with meSpeak, an espeak port, by
tools/sounds/render_speech.cjs — the audio is program output, not covered by
the tool's GPL licence):

  voice-*-1..3.mp3     cybersecurity announcer for Metasploit Framework
                       (robot female voice, light grit)
  cvoice-*.mp3         old-school computer voice for Metasploit Classic
                       (decimated + bit-crushed like an 80s speech chip)

These play as ordinary page audio, so they reach Bluetooth / a car screen
together with the rest of the site's sound.

Metasploit Classic workstation sounds (generated in build_sounds.py, no
samples): classic-key-1..4 (mechanical keyboard), classic-beep (PC speaker),
classic-error, classic-poweron (CRT degauss), classic-hum (CRT hum loop),
classic-modem (dial-up handshake), classic-glitch-1..2 (static bursts).
