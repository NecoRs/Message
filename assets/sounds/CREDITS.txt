Sound effects
=============

All files in this folder are derived from two packs by Kenney (www.kenney.nl),
released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0):
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

  Interface Sounds (1.0)  https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
  Sci-Fi Sounds           https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds

They are free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects.
Attribution is not required; it is given here as thanks.

The files were trimmed, faded, peak-normalised and encoded as mono MP3 by
tools/sounds/build_sounds.py. The two loops were made seamless with a
crossfade, and "execute" layers glitch_001 with select_002.

  type-1..5.mp3        click_002, click_003, click_004, click_005, tick_002
  tick.mp3             tick_001
  row-1, row-2.mp3     select_001, select_002
  blip.mp3             pluck_001
  execute.mp3          glitch_001 + select_002
  success-1, -2.mp3    confirmation_002, confirmation_003
  warning.mp3          question_002
  error.mp3            error_006
  sweep-1..3.mp3       maximize_004, maximize_006, door_open_000 (Sci-Fi)
  boot.mp3             force_field_000 (Sci-Fi)
  loop-processing.mp3  computer_noise_000 (Sci-Fi)
  loop-ambience.mp3    space_engine_low_001 (Sci-Fi, low-passed)
